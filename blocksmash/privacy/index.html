Block Smash: Cannon Pop — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Operator: DSG gaming ("DSG", "we", "us"). Contact: dsggamingdevs@gmail.com

This policy covers the game Block Smash: Cannon Pop (the "Game") and related support. You can play as a guest. Where available, you can link an account to back up and restore your progress. Device, app, and advertising identifiers can still be personal information.

1. Information we process

CategoryWhat and why
Game data on your deviceProgress, coins and items, settings, and similar save data. It stays on your device except for the online features listed below.
Optional sign-in, including Facebook LoginIf you choose Facebook Login, we request the public_profile permission and send the resulting sign-in token to Unity Authentication. It uses an app-specific account identifier to verify your Facebook login and link or reopen your Block Smash account. We do not request your Facebook email address. The game displays your in-game nickname and avatar. Other supported sign-in methods, such as Google, also use an account identifier to connect your game account.
Cloud backup and restoreWhen you link an account through Save Progress, we use your Unity Player ID to store and retrieve your stage and Journey progress, claimed Journey rewards, coin balance, item counts, and in-game nickname and avatar through Unity Cloud Save. This lets you restore progress on a supported device.
Online leaderboardWhere enabled, we use a game player ID, game nickname and avatar, country associated with your game profile, and stage progress to run the world and country rankings. Other players can see your game nickname, avatar, and ranking information in the relevant boards, including when you play as a guest.
AdvertisingThe Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, which may use Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and Meta Audience Network. These services may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to deliver, limit, and measure ads.
Meta (Facebook) SDKAfter you agree, the Game uses the Meta SDK to measure app installs and ad performance. It may process your advertising ID, device and app information, and basic app activity.
PurchasesPurchases are handled by Google Play. We receive purchase details to deliver items, restore purchases, and check refunds: the store and product, a transaction or purchase identifier (we store a hashed form of the purchase token), time, amount and currency, and purchase or refund status. We do not receive your payment-card details.
NotificationsIf you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders on your device. They are created locally and do not send your data to us.
SupportIf you email us, we process your email address and the information you send.

2. What we do not do

We do not sell your information. The Game does not use a third-party game analytics SDK and does not run its own gameplay analytics service.

3. Why we use information

To run the Game, connect an account, back up and restore progress, provide online rankings, deliver and restore purchases, handle refunds and support, prevent fraud and abuse, show and measure advertising, and measure how our own ads on Meta perform.

Each provider also applies its own privacy policy: Unity, Google, Meta, Supabase.

5. Your choices

  • Consent prompt. At startup, the Game asks for your choice about data collection. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown, and the Game does not request ads unless you consent. Elsewhere, if you decline, the Game keeps Meta install and ad measurement disabled and tells the advertising services that you did not consent; ads may still be shown, but they should not be personalized.
  • Change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad Privacy.
  • Device settings. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Rewarded video ads are optional; you choose whether to watch them.
  • Save Progress. You can choose whether to link an account and can sign out in Settings. Signing out stops cloud backup on that device until you reconnect. To request deletion of the existing backup and game account, use the account deletion page.

6. Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app or its data. We keep account and cloud save data to maintain the game account and provide backup and restoration. We remove this data after verifying and processing an account deletion request, subject to any required retention. Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to deliver purchases, handle refunds and disputes, and meet legal obligations. Advertising and Meta data are kept by those providers under their own policies. Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

7. International processing

Our purchase-record server runs in Seoul, South Korea, and its database in Sydney, Australia. Authentication, cloud services, advertising and measurement providers may process data in other countries. Where required, we use the safeguards that applicable law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or move your information, to withdraw consent, or to object. Email dsggamingdevs@gmail.com. To request deletion, open the Block Smash account deletion page. We may ask for extra information to verify ownership or locate records. Providers that process data for their own purposes may ask you to contact them directly. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Uninstalling the Game does not delete data already held by servers or providers.

9. Children, security, and changes

The Game is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will review it. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security. We will update this page when our practices or the law change.

Block Smash: Cannon Pop 개인정보처리방침

1. 처리하는 정보

구분내용과 목적
기기에 저장되는 게임 정보진행도, 코인·아이템, 설정 등. 아래 온라인 기능에 사용되는 정보를 제외하면 기기에 저장됩니다.
선택적 계정 연결 및 Facebook 로그인Facebook 로그인을 선택하시면 public_profile 권한을 요청하고 발급된 로그인 토큰을 Unity Authentication으로 보냅니다. Unity Authentication은 앱 전용 계정 식별자로 Facebook 로그인을 확인해 블럭 스매시 계정을 연결하거나 다시 열어 줍니다. Facebook 이메일 주소는 요청하지 않습니다. 게임에는 게임에서 설정한 닉네임과 아바타가 표시됩니다. Google 등 지원되는 다른 로그인 방식도 계정 식별자를 사용해 게임 계정을 연결합니다.
클라우드 백업 및 복원진행 상황 저장에서 계정을 연결하면 Unity Player ID를 기준으로 스테이지·저니 진행도, 수령한 저니 보상, 코인 잔액, 아이템 수량, 게임 닉네임과 아바타를 Unity Cloud Save에 저장하고 불러옵니다. 지원되는 기기에서 진행 상황을 복원할 수 있습니다.
온라인 리더보드기능이 활성화되면 게임 플레이어 ID, 닉네임과 아바타, 게임 프로필의 국가, 스테이지 진행도를 사용하여 세계·국가 순위를 운영합니다. 게스트 플레이어도 해당 순위표에 게임 닉네임, 아바타, 순위 정보가 다른 이용자에게 표시될 수 있습니다.
광고게임은 Unity LevelPlay를 통해 광고를 보여 주며 Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network를 사용할 수 있습니다. 이 서비스는 광고 제공·제한·성과 측정을 위해 광고 식별자, IP 주소, 기기·앱 정보, 광고 상호작용을 처리할 수 있습니다.
Meta(Facebook) SDK동의하신 뒤에 앱 설치와 광고 성과 측정을 위해 사용합니다. 광고 식별자, 기기·앱 정보, 기본적인 앱 활동을 처리할 수 있습니다.
구매결제는 Google Play가 처리합니다. 아이템 지급, 구매 복원, 환불 확인을 위해 스토어·상품, 거래 또는 구매 식별 정보(구매 토큰은 해시 형태로 저장), 시각, 금액·통화, 구매·환불 상태를 받습니다. 결제카드 정보는 받지 않습니다.
알림알림을 허용하시면 기기에서 알림을 예약합니다. 알림은 기기 안에서 만들어지며 당사에 정보를 보내지 않습니다.
지원이메일로 문의하시면 이메일 주소와 보내신 내용을 처리합니다.
